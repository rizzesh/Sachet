Live Guard Warning

Visual risk meter with actionable Hindi guidance warns the user before the OTP or money transfer happens, not after.

Zero Audio Storage — Uncompromising Civic Privacy

Sachet operates on a strict privacy model: raw voice audio is never stored or transmitted. Only transient text tokens are evaluated in ephemeral volatile memory. Call sessions are automatically purged after 30 minutes, or erased immediately when you hang up.
